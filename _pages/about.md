<span style="font-size: 16px;"> I am a first year DPhil student at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/) where I am advised by [Prof. Kasper Rasmussen](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/kasper.rasmussen/). My research interest lies in the general area of applied cryptography including post-quantum cryptography, multiparty computation, private information retrieval, and so on.</span>

<span style="font-size: 16px;">Before coming to Oxford, I obtained my Master's degree from [University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/) where I was advised by [Prof. Philipp Jovanovic](https://philippjovanovic.com/) and my Bachelor's degree from the [London School of Economics](https://www.lse.ac.uk/). Since then, I have worked as a researcher at the [Alan Turing Institute](https://www.turing.ac.uk/) on privacy enhanching technologies and at [CrossBar Inc.](https://www.crossbar-inc.com/) as an applied cryptographer.</span>
